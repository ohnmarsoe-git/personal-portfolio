About me

An engineer who became the person teams rely on to ship.

I began my career writing code. Later I led development teams, and now I manage end-to-end delivery of ERP and mobile projects. Because I have built software myself, I can talk with engineers in their terms and explain trade-offs to business stakeholders in theirs. This helps projects make decisions and keep moving.

At Blue Stone Solutions I manage several client projects at the same time. I lead teams of 5–10 developers and designed the training programmes that grow our full-stack and Odoo engineers. Before that, as Technical Manager at AcePlus Solutions, I improved the performance and stability of production systems and built a code review and mentoring culture.

How I run projects

  1. Clarify early. I turn vague requests into agreed scope, acceptance criteria and a realistic plan before work starts.
  2. Deliver in short cycles. Agile/Scrum sprints with visible progress, so surprises appear early and stay small.
  3. Keep stakeholders informed. Regular status updates, clear risk logs, and a fast, controlled change-request process.
  4. Quality before release. Structured UAT, sign-off and a planned production deployment, every time.
